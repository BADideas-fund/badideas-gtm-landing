The gap

Between seed and scale, most startups lose time on things that are fixable.

The ICP is too broad to close efficiently. Messaging generates interest but not revenue. The sales motion works when the founder runs it but breaks when anyone else tries. Pipeline fills up but deals don't close.

These are growth problems. They're common, they're identifiable early, and they cost you months.

What we do about it

After we invest, we run a structured system to make sure growth actually happens.

It starts with a 4-week Sales & GTM Diagnostics Sprint — a deep assessment of your sales and go-to-market state led by Suhas Ghante (3x COO, 2x 500 Startups alum, 6x advisor) and his chosen expert partners. You get a clear action plan and investor-ready material.
